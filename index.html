Overview

Open-source models remain vulnerable to completion-style attacks, and defense via fine-tuning alone shows limited generalization. We find that removing neurons associated with termination control increases harmful continuation, while neuron-level intervention to strengthen termination provides a promising direction for safer generation.

          is more likely to transition into refusal / safe completion.
        

      


      

        
Why Is IPJ Attack Related to Termination?

        

          

            IPJ attacks emerge from the interaction between sentence completion and termination signals. The key
            mechanism differs across complete and incomplete contexts as follows.
          

          
    
            
  • 
              complete text: Once a semantic unit is completed, the model can transition more easily
              to a new meaning, making a shift from harmful continuation to safer output more likely.
            
    • 
            
  • 
              incomplete text: Before the ongoing meaning is finished, redirection is harder, so
              jailbreak behavior is more likely; refusal tends to appear only after termination cues emerge.
            
    • 
            
  • 
              incomplete + no completion: If no completion/termination signal is triggered, the model
              keeps extending the same meaning, and harmful generation can persist.
